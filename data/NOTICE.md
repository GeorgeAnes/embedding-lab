# Notice for data/corpus.jsonl

`corpus.jsonl` is derived from the public docstrings of NumPy 2.4.6 and SciPy 1.17.1, extracted with Python 3.11.15 on 30 September 2026 by `python -m lab corpus` (`lab/corpus.py`). Each record holds the docstring's first paragraph (`summary`) and the docstring text with Examples sections removed (`full`). A leading signature line (ufuncs) is skipped, the heading lines of the other sections (such as `Parameters` with its underline) are dropped, and whitespace is collapsed. The wording of the remaining text is unchanged.
